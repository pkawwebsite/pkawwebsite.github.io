Registration for PKAW 2026 is handled through the PRICAI 2026 registration system. Please visit the PRICAI 2026 Registration Page and follow the instructions provided there to complete your registration.

Please note the following registration requirements for PKAW 2026:

  • The registration deadline for PKAW 2026 is 22 September 2026. Failure to complete registration by this deadline will result in the paper being removed from the proceedings.
  • If you plan to attend PKAW only, you may register at the Workshop rate. If you plan to attend the full PRICAI 2026 program, please register at the corresponding full-conference rate.
  • Student registration is accepted for PKAW. However, you may be required to provide proof of student status for verification.
  • Each accepted PKAW paper must be covered by at least one valid registration.
  • Each registration can cover one workshop paper only. Authors with multiple accepted papers should ensure that each paper is covered by a separate valid registration.
  • For paper ID, please enter 0000. For paper title, please enter PKAW 2026.
  • After completing the payment, please send a screenshot of the payment confirmation or a copy of the bank transfer receipt (image or PDF) to sqwu[at]cityu.edu.mo.
